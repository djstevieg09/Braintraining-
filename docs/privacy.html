Brainlark privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Brainlark is a brain training game for Android. We built it so that your training stays on your phone. This policy explains what that means.

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No adverts and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics. We do not collect information about how you use the app.
  • Your players, scores, charts and handwriting samples are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

Brainlark saves the player names you type, an optional year of birth (used only to compare your Brain Age with your real age), your exercise results, your daily stamps and any handwriting samples you choose to teach the app. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting a player in Settings, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it.

Microphone (optional)

If you turn on Voice answers in Settings, Brainlark asks for microphone permission and listens only while you are playing Colour Clash, to hear the colour you say. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually provided by Google), which may process audio under that provider's own privacy policy. Brainlark does not record, store or send audio to us. You can turn Voice answers off, or remove the permission in your phone's settings, at any time.

Purchases

Brainlark Full is a one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We do not see or store your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your account owns Brainlark Full, so it can unlock the extra content and let you restore it later.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Brainlark makes a picture of your result and hands it to the app you choose (for example a messaging or social app). Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. What happens next is covered by that app's own policy.

Children

Brainlark is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us using the developer email address shown on Brainlark's Google Play listing.
